# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

## Overview

Growing Gallery is designed to preserve children's artwork and memories while respecting user privacy.

## Information Collection

Growing Gallery does not collect personal information.

The developer does not collect:

- Names
- Artwork
- Stories
- Voice recordings
- Photos
- Analytics data
- Advertising identifiers

## Data Storage

User content is stored locally on the user's device.

Users may optionally synchronize content using Apple's iCloud services.

## iCloud Synchronization

When enabled, synchronization occurs through the user's personal iCloud account.

The developer does not access, store, or process synchronized content.

## Third-Party Services

Growing Gallery uses Apple-provided services:

- CloudKit
- iCloud

No advertising networks are used.

No analytics platforms are used.

## Children's Privacy

Growing Gallery is intended for use by parents and guardians to preserve artwork and memories.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please use the support page.
