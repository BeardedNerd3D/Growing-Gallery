# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does Growing Gallery require an account?

No.

## Is my data stored by the developer?

No.

All data remains on your device or within your personal iCloud account.

## Can I use Growing Gallery without iCloud?

Yes.

iCloud synchronization is optional.

## What are Voice Memories?

Voice Memories allow you to record a child describing their artwork.

## What are Albums?

Albums organize artwork into custom collections.

## What are Art Books?

Art Books combine artwork into a keepsake-style collection.

## Can I archive artwork?

Yes.

Archived artwork remains preserved and can be restored later.

## Can I export artwork?

Export features may be added in a future release.
