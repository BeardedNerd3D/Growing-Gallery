# Growing Gallery

A private home for your child's artwork, stories, and creative memories.

Growing Gallery helps families preserve artwork without the clutter by combining photos, stories, voice memories, albums, and keepsake art books into a single organized collection.

## Features

- Child Profiles
- Artwork Library
- Camera Capture
- Voice Memories
- Albums
- Art Books
- Search & Filtering
- Private iCloud Synchronization

## Privacy First

Growing Gallery does not require an account.

Your data remains stored on your device and optionally synchronized using your personal iCloud account.

The developer does not have access to your artwork, stories, photos, or voice memories.
